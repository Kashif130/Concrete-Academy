    $CT · HYPOTHETICAL, UNOFFICIAL

    Airdrop Allocation Checker

    Concrete has not announced a token, ticker, snapshot, or airdrop date. "$CT" here is a placeholder used only to model scenarios. Every number below is a hypothetical simulation based on the assumptions you enter — not a claim, promise, or prediction of any real allocation.

    Hypothetical FDV (for a USD estimate)
    Max token supply (fixed)
    1,000,000,000 $CT

    Formula: airdrop pool = 1,000,000,000 × pool%. Your share = your Bags ÷ estimated total community Bags. Your tokens = airdrop pool × your share. The "estimated total community Bags" figure is a number you supply — Concrete has never published a total Bags count, so treat this whole tool as a what-if calculator, not a forecast. Real Bags → Concrete Points conversion, snapshot rules, and eligibility criteria are entirely up to Concrete and unknown today. Check points.concrete.xyz for your actual standing.
